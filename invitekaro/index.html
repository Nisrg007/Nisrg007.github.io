Common questions

How do I change the language?

Tap the language button (文A) at the top of the home screen. Templates in your language are shown first.

How do I unlock a premium template or remove the watermark?

Tap the points button on the home screen and watch a short ad to earn points. Spend them to unlock a premium template for good, or to save one invitation without the watermark. InviteKaro has no in-app purchases.

I changed phones. Where are my points and designs?

Points, unlocked templates and designs are saved only on the phone you made them on, so they don't move to a new phone and are removed if you uninstall the app.

My invitation didn't save to the gallery.

Allow InviteKaro to add photos in your phone's settings (Settings → InviteKaro → Photos), then tap Save again.

Delete your data

Your designs, photos, points and unlocked templates are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

The app also creates an anonymous account ID on our servers (no name, email or phone number). To delete it, email nisargwork2004@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We delete it within 30 days.
