Privacy Policy

InviteKaro · Effective date: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what information the InviteKaro mobile app ("InviteKaro", "the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. InviteKaro is published by STREEFI PRIVATE LIMITED, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.

The short version: you do not need to create an account, your photos and designs stay on your phone, and we collect only what we need to run the app and show ads. InviteKaro has no in-app purchases.

1. Information we collect

a) Information that stays on your device

  • Photos you add to a design. When you pick a photo from your gallery, it is used only inside your design on your phone. We do not upload your photos to our servers.
  • Your designs and drafts, favourites, language choice, your reward points balance and the premium templates you have unlocked. These are saved in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Exported invitations are saved to your photo gallery, or shared through the app you choose (for example WhatsApp), only when you tap Save or Share.

b) Information we store on our servers

  • An anonymous account ID. On first launch the app creates a random user ID, without asking for your name, email or phone number. We use it to load templates and to give our own team access to publish templates.

c) Information collected by third-party services

  • Advertising (Google AdMob). Free users see ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA or UK), we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts). The app downloads fonts from Google's servers the first time they are needed. Like any web request, this shares your IP address with Google.

We do not collect your contacts, precise location, name, email address or phone number.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the app: templates, editing, saving and exporting invitations.
  • To offer rewarded ads that earn points, and to unlock premium templates or remove the watermark when you spend them.
  • To show ads to free users.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Permissions

  • Photos / gallery: to add your photos to a design and to save finished invitations.
  • Internet: to load templates, fonts and ads.

You can turn permissions off at any time in your phone's settings; related features will stop working.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device, including your points and unlocked templates, is removed when you delete a design or uninstall the app.
  • To delete your anonymous account, email us at nisargwork2004@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We will complete the request within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other laws that apply to you (such as the GDPR), you may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate another person to exercise your rights. You can also opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). Contact us using the details below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India or your local authority.

7. Children

InviteKaro is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add new features or services. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
